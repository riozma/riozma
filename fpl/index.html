Is the FPL for you?

If you enjoy developing new ideas for more efficient and better planning with others, you are in the right place. Primarily civil engineering Master's students, but open to everyone interested. Take away what you like: input for your projects, ideas for theses, maybe a startup, or simply a relaxed round of deeper thinking with like-minded people. No commitment needed, join only the sessions that interest you.
