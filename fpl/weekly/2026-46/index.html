AI agents move into the BIM model, robots patrol Japanese sites, 3D-printed housing reaches Portugal

Qonic put AI agents directly into its cloud BIM platform and opened it to outside AI assistants, while a German overview shows where MCP servers for Revit, Archicad and Allplan stand. Kajima is rolling out autonomous robots on more than 10 Japanese sites. A cement group launched a 3D construction brand after printing 32 homes in Portugal, and the US Air Force wants mobile concrete printers. In Switzerland, SIA 405 gets one register for all utility operators. Our focus: 3D printing in construction.

  • Qonic builds AI agents into its cloud BIM platform and lets outside assistants such as Claude work in the model (AEC Magazine)
  • Japanese contractor Kajima rolls out self-navigating robots on more than 10 sites after 2,000 autonomous missions (Kajima Corporation press release (PDF))
  • Cement maker CIMPOR launches a 3D construction brand after printing 32 homes in Portugal with low-carbon concrete (World Cement)
  • SIA 405: one Swiss register replaces dozens of spellings of who owns a pipe or cable (Espazium (TEC21 / SIA))
  • Which CAD and BIM programs can AI assistants already use? A German overview of MCP servers from Revit to Allplan (Internet für Architekten (German))
  • Cement-free pavers made with steel slag and CO2 cover 6,400 m² at an Amazon depot in England (CarbiCrete press release)
  • SBB closes a track near Biel because the railway embankment is sinking into peat and lake marl (Baublatt (German))
  • US Air Force asks for mobile 3D concrete printers that lightly trained crews can use to rebuild damaged bases (Defense News)
