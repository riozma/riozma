Robots get funded, geotech goes digital, concrete goes round

Investors poured money into construction robotics this week, from ICON's 3D printers to Origin's wall-finishing robot. Hexagon is buying the geotechnical software house Rocscience to connect sensors with simulation, and recycled concrete got one of the biggest circular-economy rounds of the year. Our focus this week: what BIM really is, and why it is not yet a digital twin.

  • ICON raises $200M to build multi-storey 3D-printing robots (Construction Dive)
  • Origin comes out of stealth with $20M for an interior-finishing robot (Robotics & Automation News)
  • Four-legged robots will help build a housing project in Koblenz (Stacker)
  • Hexagon buys Rocscience for $535M to connect sensors and simulation (Hexagon (press release))
  • McKinsey: AI could automate about 39% of non-physical construction work (Stacker)
  • SL Materials gets €100M to turn demolition concrete into new concrete (ESG Today)
  • EU project REVAIL starts: reclaimed building parts for renovations (Eurac Research)
  • SBB is building a digital twin of the Swiss rail network (Axiom)
